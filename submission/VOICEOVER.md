# Demo video — voice-over script

Video: `submission/porchlight-demo.mp4` (1:50, silent). Each line below is timed to what is on screen. Read calmly, about 140 words per minute. If a line runs long, leave out the part in *(brackets)*.

Tip: in CapCut / Clipchamp, put the video on the timeline, press **Record voice-over**, and read while you watch. Two takes is plenty.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|

| **1:38 – 1:43** | “For how long?” 45 min | “Choose how long, and your light fades on its own.” |
| **1:43 – 1:50** | “Your light is on”, Dev is coming | “Your light is on, and someone’s already on their way. Porchlight: leave a light on.” |
