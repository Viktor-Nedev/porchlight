01 · Concept & metaphor

Light = openness. The whole brand is warm windows in the dark.

Wick, a shy paper lantern, is the mascot and the emotional arc of the product: dim when alone, glowing when someone says hi. The story site moves from dusk to night over Calgary, so the city literally lights up as the story unfolds.

Every visual choice supports one feeling: calm, hopeful, never pushy. No gamified badges, no follower counts, no bright notification reds.

06 · Components & interaction rules

Lantern rating scale

Five lanterns from Awkward to Great; glow and expression grow with the score. Used twice (forecast and reality) so the comparison is visual, not numeric.

Person pin

Teardrop in the person’s colour, initial on a paper face, a small vibe badge (food, study, coffee, company, walk). Selected pins scale up 18%.

Time ring

A thin amber ring around each avatar shows time left on that light, so urgency is visible without a countdown.

Seat indicator

Filled squares for taken seats, outlined for free ones; the detail sheet shows the same as a small table diagram.

Spark card

Flips on tap (0.8 s ease-out); “Another Spark” swaps the text in place. Both people always get the same card.

Lamp button

The centre of the tab bar; breathes slowly so the primary action is always discoverable.

Motion

Ease-out (0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), 0.45 s screen transitions; everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Accessibility

Text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, labelled icons, keyboard-operable pins and forms, colour never carries meaning alone.

Safety

Shield always in the header; “I have to run” on every in-progress screen; lights snap to public spots only.

07 · Story site
Hero at dusk: five painted parallax layers
Scroll to night: windows light up, Wick finds a friend
The in-between hour

Interactions: pull-chain preloader, dusk-to-night colour grade, pinned storytelling, C-Train arrival boards with split-flap text, a window that opens onto the campus, a sticky phone that drives the live prototype, taped postcards with 3D tilt, a clickable constellation, a horizontal transit-line roadmap and rising lanterns.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript (GSAP, Lenis). No build step.
